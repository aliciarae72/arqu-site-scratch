Programs

See how we build custom capacity for your book and find the economies of scale inside it.

01

Book roll

We are your program builder and administrator, not your wholesaler. We start from your book and fit capacity to it, not the other way around. Priced as one book, the spread of risk works in everyone's favor.

  • InsuredsLower, more competitive price than the open market gives one risk at a time.
  • RetailersScale your book without scaling your payroll. We have consolidated nearly 100 markets into one.
  • Capacity providersA ready-to-roll book, pre-underwritten and delivered in bulk. Not a business plan.
02

Custom capacity and administration

We source capacity for the program, including fronted solutions, and we administer it. Importantly, we let capacity providers do what they do best — underwrite — and we do what we do best — distribute.

  • InsuredsNot your traditional layered and shared program. Each insured gets their own policy and their own limit.
  • RetailersNew carriers you have not used, and new underwriting units inside the ones you know. Quotes come straight from the agreed rates, so binding takes minutes, not weeks.
  • Capacity providersHand us any part of the process, or all of it. Quoting, binding, policy issuance, invoicing: we built the tools that lift the admin load.

The result: a program you can market as your own, like having your own MGA.
