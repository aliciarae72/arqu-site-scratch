The read behind every submission

The headline says no. The record says otherwise.

Underwriters see the headline first: a pipeline in fire country, a building in hail country. We go past it. We pull the public record, build the chart, and put the answer in the submission before anyone asks for it.

The headline: pipelines start wildfires. The record since 2010: 1,349 pipeline ignitions, 3,324 wildfires within ten miles of one, and one wildfire a pipeline directly caused.

The headline: Colorado is hail country. The record, cell by cell: nearly all of the state's 11,026 grid cells rate very low. The severe cells crowd the Front Range and the eastern plains.
