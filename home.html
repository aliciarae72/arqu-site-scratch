01

Book roll

We package your book into one programmatic placement. Priced as one book, the spread of risk works in everyone's favor.

  • InsuredsLower, more competitive price than the open market gives one risk at a time.
  • RetailersLess admin and more time to win new business. We have consolidated nearly 100 markets into one.
  • Capacity providersOne book to underwrite instead of hundreds of one-off submissions.
02

Custom capacity and administration

We source capacity for the program, including fronted solutions, and we administer it. Importantly, we let capacity providers do what they do best — underwrite — and we do what we do best — distribute.

  • InsuredsNot your traditional layered and shared program. Each insured gets their own policy and their own limit.
  • RetailersQuotes come straight from the agreed rates, so binding takes minutes, not weeks.
  • Capacity providersWe do whatever lifting is required of us. Quoting, binding, policy issuance, invoicing: we have built the tools to make deploying capacity easy.

The result: a program you can market as your own.
